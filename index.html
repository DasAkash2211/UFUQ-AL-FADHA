HOW WE WORK

Our Working Process

A clear, step-by-step approach we follow for every duct and coil cleaning job to ensure clean, safe and reliable results.

AC Duct Cleaning Process

  1. Removing A/C grills/diffusers and washing them.
  2. Complete cleaning of A/C ducts from inside using a high power duct cleaning machine.
  3. After cleaning the A/C ducts, antibacterial fumigation spray will be made for the ducts.
  4. We make sure all furniture and items inside the apartment/villa are covered with plastic sheets to keep them intact and dust free.
  5. A/C unit filters will be cleaned along with this A/C duct cleaning service.
  6. Before and after pictures along with a report will be shared after 24 hours.

Coils Cleaning Process

  1. Check access to coil – remove ceiling tile.
  2. Cover up the floor, furniture and anything in the area of the FCU — this is very important and must be double checked when in a customer's house.
  3. Remove covers/housing/filters.
  4. Drip tray removed & cleaned thoroughly.
  5. Blower fan cleaning.
  6. Coils cleaning.
  7. Drain tray and drain line cleaning.
  8. Filters cleaning.
